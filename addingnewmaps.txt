MOVED
=====

The game-adding guide now lives at docs/ADDING_GAMES.md and follows the
single-source-of-truth registry in packages/shared/src/games.ts (GAME_REGISTRY).

Add one manifest there, drop the asset files in apps/web/public/, then:

  pnpm --filter @taibled/shared build
  cd apps/api && pnpm validate-game <sentinel> && pnpm seed:dev

See docs/ADDING_GAMES.md for the full runbook.
