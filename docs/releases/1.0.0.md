Searchlight 1.0 is the app @JulzyWulzy built from May to September 2026 on top of [JetLagHideAndSeek](https://github.com/taibeled/JetLagHideAndSeek) by @taibeled and its contributors, the map for playing Hide and Seek from Jet Lag: The Game. JetLagHideAndSeek brought the map and its questions; Searchlight 1.0 turned it into one shared app for the Jet Lag community's games.

It covers the work up to 26 September 2026, and was published later, when Searchlight started numbering its releases. Every change since JetLagHideAndSeek is in the [list of commits](https://github.com/Jetlag-Europa/Searchlight/compare/8341a2f2a2d9cca6b7f6f3118231f1e342977cc8...v1.0.0).

## For players

- Log in with Discord and play the games of your Discord servers.
- Sessions are saved on the server, so your questions, drawings and hider mode follow you to any device.
- Play together live: everyone in a session sees changes as they happen, and changes made offline are sent once you're back.
- Invite people to a session, or send them a private copy of it.
- **Pen Mode** for drawing on the map.
- Hiding zones can show their names, and single questions can be turned on and off.

## Games

- 21 games across the JetLag UK, EU, BNL, CH and Nordics servers and Home Games, each with its own hiding zones and hiding radius, and many with questions of their own.
- Server admins choose on `/admin` which of their Discord roles may manage games.
