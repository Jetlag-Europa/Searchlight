Searchlight 2.1 can be installed as an app and keeps working through a bad connection. It also adds Google Maps as a base map, spectator links, and a history of every question change.

Every change since [2.0](https://github.com/Jetlag-Europa/Searchlight/releases/tag/v2.0.0) is in the [list of commits](https://github.com/Jetlag-Europa/Searchlight/compare/v2.0.0...v2.1.0).

## For players

- **Install Searchlight as an app:** **Install app** in Chrome and Edge, or **Share → Add to Home Screen** on iPhone and iPad. On an iPhone you may have to log in once more inside the app.
- **Bad connections:** your session and its game still open when the server can't be reached, map areas you've looked at stay on your device, and your changes are sent once the connection is back. A session that can't load shows the copy on your device and catches up by itself.
- **New versions wait for you:** "A new version of Searchlight is available" with a **Reload** button, instead of the page changing mid-game.
- **Google Maps** as a base map (Options → Base map style): Roadmap, Satellite, Hybrid or Terrain, with public transport lines, and an option to hide shops and places so footpaths stand out. Google doesn't allow storing its map, so pick CARTO Voyager where the connection is weak.
- **Spectator links:** a session's creator can share a link to follow the board live without changing anything. Spectators never see the hider's position, and the creator can turn a player into a spectator and back.
- **Question history:** **History**, at the bottom of the Questions sidebar, shows who added, answered, changed or deleted each question, and when. **Restore** brings back a deleted question long after its Undo is gone.
- **Nobody's question gets overwritten:** when you and another player change the same question differently, Searchlight asks which version to keep. Everything else merges on its own.
- **Faster on big games:** question cards stay usable while the map works out the remaining area, and the hiding-zone map no longer freezes with Zone Labels on or with No Overlap.
- A session is changed in one tab at a time. The other tab says so and offers **Use here**.
- Deleting a session asks first, and on phones Delete and Leave are always visible.
- Matching questions name what's closest to the pin, such as "Closest: Meiji Shrine".
- When the browser blocks the clipboard, the paste buttons open a field to paste into. Pasting a coordinate pair or a Google Maps link into Latitude or Longitude sets both.
- Fixes:
  - Custom points show their blue pin instead of a broken image.
  - Options → Hider Location now shows its Save button when auto-save is off.
  - The map's buttons no longer stop responding after a confirm closes.
  - The zone sidebar no longer disappears with No Overlap.
  - A zone you're viewing that an answer rules out now shows the zones left, instead of an error.
  - A toast's close button no longer overlaps its Undo.

## For game makers

- Rename a game or change its size from `/games` → **Settings**, without importing its file again. Sessions playing it are offered the update.
- **Casual Games**, for games outside the JetLag servers: people with the role an admin chooses can upload their own games there. Casual games are unlisted, and sessions start from game links their creator makes.
- Importing a file whose `id` is already taken says which game it will update. If the file looks like a different game, the import page asks before replacing it.
- Game links posted in Discord, WhatsApp, Telegram and similar apps preview as the game's name and a picture of its map.

## Removed

- `/designer`: the import page, `/games/import`, does everything it did. Old links open the import page, which needs you to be logged in.
- The map's print button, which had stopped working. Your browser's own print still works, but leaves out Google's map.
