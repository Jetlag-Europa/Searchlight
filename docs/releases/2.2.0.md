Searchlight 2.2 adds local play without signing in, and a Map Creator that builds a game in the browser. It also saves a session's questions to a file, and shares disabled hiding zones with everyone in the session.

Every change since [2.1](https://github.com/Jetlag-Europa/Searchlight/releases/tag/v2.1.0) is in the [list of commits](https://github.com/Jetlag-Europa/Searchlight/compare/v2.1.0...v2.2.0).

## For players

- **Play locally, without signing in:** load a game file or link into a board that lives only in this browser, from **Play locally without signing in** on the home page or **Play locally on this device** on a game link. Local sessions aren't synced or shared.
- **Save questions to a file:** Options → **Export Questions** and **Import Questions**.
- **Disabled zones are shared:** zones you disable by hand now show for everyone in the session, and on your other devices.
- **Faster in long sessions:** adding a question updates the map in about a second instead of 15 to 20.
- **You stay logged in** while you use Searchlight, instead of being logged out 30 days after logging in.
- Fixes:
  - The map no longer fails with "No solutions found" near the play area's border.
  - Logging in with Discord on iPhone no longer fails with "Failed to exchange OAuth code".
  - **Share a copy** no longer shows the previous session's links.

## For game makers

- **Map Creator** (**Create a map** on the home page, or **Map Creator** on `/games`): pick a play area, fetch stations and places, or import KML, GeoJSON or CSV, then download a `.game.json` or Google My Maps file, test it locally, or import it. **Edit in Creator** on `/games` opens an existing game.
- A file's `id` only has to be unique on its server, and **Replace with file…** on `/games` replaces a game with any file.
- **Export JSON** includes the game's `id`, so a re-imported file updates the game instead of adding a copy.
- Validation errors say what to change.
- Server admins see who changed what in each server's **Activity** tab on `/admin`.
